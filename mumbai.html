Welcome to Mumbai

City of Dreams & Maximum City

About Mumbai

Mumbai, the financial capital of India, is a vibrant metropolis known for its bustling city life, Bollywood film industry, and historical landmarks. From the iconic Gateway of India to the beautiful Marine Drive, Mumbai offers a perfect blend of colonial heritage and modern development.

Best Time to Visit

November to February

Famous For

Bollywood, Beaches

Language

Marathi, Hindi, English

Famous Places in Mumbai

Taj Mahal Palace Hotel

Iconic luxury hotel overlooking the Gateway of India, known for its stunning architecture and rich history dating back to 1903.

Open 24 Hours Luxury Stay

Marine Drive

Beautiful 3.6 km long boulevard along the coast, famously known as the Queen's Necklace due to its curved shape and street lights.

Open 24 Hours Free Entry

Gateway of India

Historic monument built in 1924, standing at 26 meters tall. It's Mumbai's most famous landmark and a popular tourist attraction.

Open 24 Hours Free Entry

Travel Tips for Mumbai

Local Transport

Use local trains for quick travel between suburbs. Auto-rickshaws and taxis are easily available. Download transport apps for convenience.

Street Food

Don't miss Mumbai's famous street food like vada pav, pav bhaji, and bhel puri. Try them at popular joints for authentic taste.

Weather

Carry umbrella during monsoon (June-September). Winters are pleasant, summers can be humid. Light cotton clothes recommended.

Sightseeing

Visit Gateway of India, Marine Drive, and Colaba Causeway. Explore South Mumbai for historical buildings and architecture.
