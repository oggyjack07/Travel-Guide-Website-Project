Welcome to Delhi

Capital City & Historical Marvel

About Delhi

Delhi, the capital of India, is a city where ancient history and modern development coexist beautifully. From magnificent Mughal monuments to bustling markets and diplomatic enclaves, Delhi offers a rich tapestry of culture, history, and contemporary urban life.

Best Time to Visit

October to March

Famous For

History, Food, Markets

Language

Hindi, English, Punjabi

Famous Places in Delhi

India Gate

Iconic war memorial dedicated to Indian soldiers who died in World War I. Beautifully lit in evenings and surrounded by lush lawns.

Open 24 Hours Free Entry

Red Fort

Historic fort built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1639. UNESCO World Heritage Site and venue for Independence Day celebrations.

9:30AM - 4:30PM ₹35 Indians

Qutub Minar

Tallest brick minaret in the world, built in 1193. UNESCO World Heritage Site with intricate carvings and surrounding historical complex.

7AM - 5PM ₹35 Indians

Travel Tips for Delhi

Metro Travel

Use Delhi Metro for convenient and affordable travel across the city. Get a smart card for discounted fares and easy access.

Street Food

Try Delhi's famous street food like chole bhature, parathas, and chaat. Visit Chandni Chowk for authentic local flavors.

Monument Visits

Visit historical monuments early morning to avoid crowds and heat. Carry water and wear comfortable walking shoes.

Shopping

Explore markets like Dilli Haat, Janpath, and Sarojini Nagar for handicrafts, clothes, and souvenirs at reasonable prices.
