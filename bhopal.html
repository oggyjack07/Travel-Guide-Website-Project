Welcome to Bhopal

City of Lakes & Cultural Heritage

About Bhopal

Bhopal, the capital city of Madhya Pradesh, is famously known as the City of Lakes for its various natural and artificial lakes. The city beautifully blends history, culture, and modernity with its Islamic and Hindu influences, historic monuments, and vibrant local markets.

Best Time to Visit

October to March

Famous For

Lakes, History

Language

Hindi, Urdu

Famous Places in Bhopal

Upper Lake (Bhojtal)

One of Asia's largest artificial lakes, built in the 11th century. Perfect for boating, evening walks, and enjoying beautiful sunsets over the water.

6AM - 8PM Boating ₹100-300

Taj-ul-Masajid

One of the largest mosques in Asia, known for its magnificent pink facade, huge courtyard, and stunning architecture from the 19th century.

6AM - 8PM Free Entry

Bharat Bhavan

Renowned multi-arts complex and museum showcasing contemporary and tribal art, theater performances, and cultural events by the lake.

2PM - 8PM ₹30 Entry

Travel Tips for Bhopal

Lake Activities

Enjoy boating in Upper Lake during early morning or evening. Carry sunscreen and hats for daytime lake visits.

Historical Sites

Visit museums and historical sites in the morning to avoid crowds. Wear comfortable shoes for exploring.

Local Markets

Explore New Market for handicrafts and local products. Try Bhopali street food like poha and samosa.

Weather

Winters are pleasant for sightseeing. Summers can be hot, so plan indoor activities during afternoons.
