Welcome to Dewas

City of Hills & Spiritual Serenity

About Dewas

Dewas is a peaceful city known for its scenic hills and spiritual significance. Famous for the Tekri Mata Temple situated atop a hill, it offers breathtaking panoramic views of the city. The city has a rich historical background and is known for its industrial growth while maintaining its natural beauty and cultural heritage.

Best Time to Visit

October to March

Famous For

Hill Temples, Nature

Language

Hindi, Malwi

Famous Places in Dewas

Tekri Mata Temple

Famous hilltop temple dedicated to Goddess Durga, offering spectacular views of Dewas city. The temple is accessible by stairs and ropeway.

6AM - 8PM Free Entry

Meetha Talab

Meetha Talab in Dewas is a peaceful and popular lake for family visits. Surrounded by nature, it offers morning walks, boating, and relaxing evenings with lights and soft music.

7AM - 7PM Free Entry

Kailadevi Temple

Beautiful temple complex with intricate architecture, dedicated to Goddess Kailadevi. Popular among locals and tourists for peace and devotion.

6AM - 9PM Free Entry

Travel Tips for Dewas

Hill Climbing

Wear comfortable shoes for climbing Tekri hill. Early morning or evening is best to avoid heat. Carry water during the climb.

Photography

Tekri hill offers amazing panoramic views of Dewas. Best photography time is during sunrise and sunset for stunning shots.

Weather Preparedness

Carry umbrella during monsoon. Winters can be chilly, especially in early mornings and evenings on the hill.

Local Shopping

Buy local handicrafts and pooja items from shops near temples. Try local snacks from street vendors.
