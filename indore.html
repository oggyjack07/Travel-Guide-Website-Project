Welcome to Indore

India's Cleanest City & Food Capital

About Indore

Indore, the largest city in Madhya Pradesh, is famous for its delicious street food, vibrant markets, and rich cultural heritage. Known as India's cleanest city, it offers a perfect blend of modernity and tradition with historical palaces, bustling markets, and amazing culinary experiences.

Best Time to Visit

October to March

Famous For

Street Food, Shopping

Language

Hindi, Malwi

Famous Places in Indore

Rajwada Palace

Historic palace of the Holkar dynasty, showcasing Maratha architecture with seven stories structure built in the 18th century.

9AM - 5PM ₹25 Entry

Sarafa Bazaar

Famous jewelry market by day that transforms into a street food paradise at night with numerous food stalls.

6PM - 2AM Free Entry

Lal Bagh Palace

Grand palace spread over 28 acres, featuring European architecture and antique collections from the Holkar era.

10AM - 5PM ₹50 Entry

Travel Tips for Indore

Food Experience

Don't miss Sarafa Bazaar at night for amazing street food. Try Poha-Jalebi for breakfast and Bhutte ka kees as local specialty.

Shopping

Visit Sitlamata Bazaar for traditional textiles, Sarafa for jewelry, and Malwa Mill for cotton fabrics.

Transport

Auto-rickshaws are convenient for short distances. Use app-based taxis for longer routes. City buses available for budget travel.

Best Time

October to March for pleasant weather. Avoid summer afternoons. Monsoon brings greenery but occasional showers.
