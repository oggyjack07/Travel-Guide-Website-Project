Welcome to Ujjain

Spiritual City on the Banks of Shipra River

About Ujjain

Ujjain, one of the seven sacred cities of Hinduism, is famous for the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga temple. Located on the banks of Shipra River, it's a major pilgrimage site and hosts the Simhastha Kumbh Mela every 12 years. The city has rich historical significance dating back to ancient times and is mentioned in various Hindu scriptures.

Best Time to Visit

October to March

Famous For

Spirituality, Temples

Language

Hindi, Malwi

Famous Places in Ujjain

Mahakaleshwar Temple

One of the 12 Jyotirlingas, famous for its Bhasma Aarti performed early morning. The temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and attracts millions of devotees annually.

4AM - 11PM Free Entry

Ram Ghat

Sacred bathing ghat on Shipra River, especially crowded during Kumbh Mela. Pilgrims take holy dips here believing it washes away their sins.

Open 24 Hours Free Entry

Kal Bhairav Temple

Ancient temple dedicated to Kal Bhairav, the guardian deity of Ujjain. Known for unique tradition of offering alcohol to the deity.

6AM - 9PM Free Entry

Travel Tips for Ujjain

Temple Etiquette

Remove footwear before entering temples. Dress modestly and maintain silence in temple premises. Follow temple timings for darshan.

Holy Dip

Take a holy dip in Shipra River at Ram Ghat during auspicious occasions. Early morning is the best time for spiritual experience.

Bhasma Aarti

Don't miss the famous Bhasma Aarti at Mahakaleshwar Temple. Reach early as it gets very crowded. Photography may be restricted.

Shopping

Buy religious items, pooja samagri, and local handicrafts from markets near temples. Bargain politely at local shops.
